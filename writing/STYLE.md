# Writing style
- Write in English for an international academic audience.
- Make one point clearly rather than translating or summarizing whole sources.
- Keep posts short (normally 400–700 words).
- First-person claims must derive from the author's own notes, not model invention.
- Preserve source URLs. Label uncertainty and check citations.
- Do not expose unpublished manuscripts, identifiable personal data, credentials, or private annotations verbatim.
- AI proposes; the author decides. All generated posts remain Hugo drafts until approved.
