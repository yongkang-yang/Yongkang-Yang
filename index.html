Yongkang Yang

I'm a PhD candidate in Organizational Behavior at Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University Rotterdam, supervised by Prof. Daan van Knippenberg.

My research focuses on team membership change, multicultural teams, team diversity, and information elaboration. I use longitudinal and social network approaches to study how teams adapt and perform in dynamic contexts. I also study how artificial intelligence shapes people, teams, and organizations.
