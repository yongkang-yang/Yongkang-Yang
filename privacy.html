Privacy Policy — johancli

Last updated: 25 October 2026

johancli is a personal command-line tool operated by Yongkang Yang for his own use. It is not offered to the public, and only the owner's own Google accounts are authorized to sign in.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app may access data in the owner's Google accounts, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks and Contacts, solely to perform tasks the owner requests (for example, summarizing email, creating calendar events, or reading and saving files).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and used only to provide features to the owner.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
