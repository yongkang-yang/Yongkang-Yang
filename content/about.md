I'm a PhD candidate in Organizational Behavior at [Rotterdam School of Management](https://www.rsm.nl/), Erasmus University Rotterdam, supervised by [Prof. Daan van Knippenberg](https://business.rice.edu/person/daan-van-knippenberg).

My research focuses on [team membership change, multicultural teams, team diversity, and information elaboration](https://www.erim.eur.nl/people/yongkang-yang/). I use longitudinal and social network approaches to study how teams adapt and perform in dynamic contexts. I also study how artificial intelligence shapes people, teams, and organizations.

In January–April 2026 I was a Visiting Scholar in Management and Organization at Penn State University.
